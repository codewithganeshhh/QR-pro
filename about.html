About QR Pro

A modern, QR-powered customer management platform built for businesses of all sizes.

Our Vision

To make every business interaction smarter by replacing paper business cards with instant, scannable digital profiles.

Our Mission

To give businesses a fast, simple, and professional way to manage customer data and share contact information with a single QR scan.

Who We Serve

Freelancers, small businesses, enterprises — anyone who meets clients and wants to make a lasting first impression.

Built with modern technology, designed for real people.

QR Pro was created to solve a simple problem: when you meet a customer, sharing your details should take one second — not ten. We built a system that lets you generate a unique QR code per customer, and when that QR is scanned, it opens a beautifully designed digital profile card instantly.
